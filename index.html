    How to play

    1✋

    Raise your hand so the whole hand is in view.

    2⏱️

    Throw on “Shoot!” and hold the shape for a moment.

    3💥

    Win the exchange to hit. Empty their bar for a K.O.

    Who wins

    ✊ Rock beats ✌️ Scissors
    ✌️ Scissors beat ✋ Paper
    ✋ Paper beats ✊ Rock

    Win streaks hit harder. First to 2 rounds wins the match. Too slow, or an illegal shape (one finger, three fingers), loses the throw.

    Power-ups

    • 👍
      Shield — blocks one hit. Everyone gets one per round.
    • 👉
      Power — ×2 damage. Earned by winning 2 in a row.
    • 🤘
      Reverse — the loser deals the damage. Earned by losing 2 in a row.

    Hold the sign between throws to arm it; it fires on your next throw.
